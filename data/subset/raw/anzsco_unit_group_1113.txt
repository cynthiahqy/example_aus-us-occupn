UNIT GROUP 1113 LEGISLATORS

Definition:
LEGISLATORS represent the interests of people in constituencies as elected members of national, state and territory parliaments and local government authorities.

Indicative Skill Level:
Occupations require a level of skill commensurate with a bachelor degree or higher qualification. At least five years of relevant experience may substitute for the formal qualification (ANZSCO Skill Level 1).

Tasks Include:
- Developing policy and formulating legislation at various government levels
- Assisting constituents with government-related matters
- Investigating public concerns
- Introducing proposals representing electoral interests
- Issuing policy directions and exercising control
- Attending community functions and gauging public opinion
- Serving on parliamentary committees
- Presenting petitions for concerned groups

Occupations:

111311 LOCAL GOVERNMENT LEGISLATOR
Alternative titles: Alderman, Councillor
Represents constituency interests as elected local government member.
Specialisations include Mayor and Community Board Member (NZ).

111312 MEMBER OF PARLIAMENT
Alternative title: Parliamentarian
Represents constituency interests as elected national, state, or territory parliament member.
Specialisations include Prime Minister, Premier, Minister, and Senator.

111399 LEGISLATORS NEC
Covers legislators not elsewhere classified, including Aboriginal Community Council Members and Councillors of Aboriginal Land Councils (Aus).
