UNIT GROUP 1111 CHIEF EXECUTIVES AND MANAGING DIRECTORS

Definition:
CHIEF EXECUTIVES AND MANAGING DIRECTORS determine, formulate and review the general policy programs and the overall direction of organisations within the framework established by boards of directors and similar governing bodies.

Indicative Skill Level:
Most positions require bachelor degree or higher, with at least five years relevant experience potentially substituting for formal qualifications (ANZSCO Skill Level 1).

Tasks Include:
- Determining objectives, strategies, policies and programs
- Providing overall direction and management
- Authorizing material, human and financial resources
- Monitoring organizational performance
- Consulting with senior staff
- Preparing reports, budgets and forecasts
- Representing the organization externally
- Selecting or approving senior staff selection
- Ensuring legal compliance

Occupation (6-digit code):

111111 Chief Executive or Managing Director
Alternative title: Chief Executive Officer
This role involves determining, formulating and reviewing general policy and overall organizational direction within board-established frameworks.
Specialisations include: Director-General, Executive Director, Secretary (Government Department).
