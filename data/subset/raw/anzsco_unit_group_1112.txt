Unit Group 1112: General Managers

Definition:
GENERAL MANAGERS plan, organise, direct, control and review the day-to-day operations and major functions of commercial, industrial, government and defence organisations through departmental managers and subordinate executives.

Indicative Skill Level:
Most occupations require a level of skill commensurate with a bachelor degree or higher qualification. At least five years of relevant experience may substitute for the formal qualification (ANZSCO Skill Level 1).

Tasks Include:
- Planning policy and setting organizational standards and objectives
- Providing day-to-day direction and managing operations
- Assessing changing situations and issuing directives to staff
- Consulting with subordinates on operational matters
- Authorizing funding for major programs
- Representing the organization at official occasions and negotiations
- Preparing reports, budgets and forecasts for governing bodies
- Selecting and managing senior staff performance
- Overseeing accounting, sales, marketing, and human resources functions

Occupations:

111211 Corporate General Manager
Alternative Title: Chief Operating Officer
Directs operations of commercial, industrial, or governmental organizations through departmental managers.
Specialisations include Assistant Commissioner (Police), Hospital Administrator, Managing Editor, and Trade Union Secretary.

111212 Defence Force Senior Officer
Alternative Titles: Air Force Senior Officer, Army Senior Officer, Navy Senior Officer
Directs operations of Defence Force organizational units.
Specialisations span multiple ranks including General, Admiral, Air Chief Marshal, and Brigadier.
